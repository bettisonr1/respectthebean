# Roadmap

Living backlog for **respectthebean** — ideas and planned directions. Order does not imply priority.

## Features

1. **Intelligent suggestions for the next shot** — Given the last shot’s result (taste, ratios, etc.), suggest concrete adjustments: grind, dose in, yield, temperature, or workflow tweaks.

2. **Rate the shot from ratios** — Score or explain quality based on achieved ratios (e.g. dose : yield, extraction metrics) relative to targets or style.

3. **Computer vision → latte art rating** — Analyze uploaded photos for symmetry, contrast, and complexity; suggest improvements. Builds on existing artwork upload / analysis flow.

4. **Analytics** — Graph views such as grind vs yield, dose vs taste rating, and other correlations over time.

5. **Social layer** — Friends, feed of others’ shots, brew streaks, bean challenges, “clone” others’ recipes, upvotes.

6. **Coffee map** — Map of nearby specialty cafés. Users log what they drank at a venue; appears in their feed and the café’s feed.

7. **Gamification** — Achievements; unlock features or badges based on usage and milestones.

8. **Hardware integration** — Connect smart scales (and similar devices) for frictionless shot logging.

9. **Coffee journal** — Daily diary: mood, notes, photos; explore mood ↔ coffee correlations.

10. **Community benchmarks** — Aggregate insights like “people with your machine and this bean often use X grind / Y ratio” (privacy-preserving, opt-in where needed).
